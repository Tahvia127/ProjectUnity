Social Impact Incubator

Y-PHIC

Youth Public Health Innovation Coalition - where youth-led public health organizations are built. We work with early-stage founders to sharpen their model, strengthen operations, and connect them to communities that carry their work forward.

Apply to the IncubatorMeet the Cohort
Our Belief

The best builders in public health are already out there. They just need the right room.

Y-PHIC is a program of Project UNITY, launched with a pilot grant from the Common Health Coalition. Through 6 months of incubation, we help nonprofits refine their missions and scale their operations, backed by public health mentors, startup operators, and healthcare practitioners.

Frequently Asked Questions

About Y-PHIC

What does Y-PHIC stand for?
Youth Public Health Innovation Coalition. A social impact incubator and program of Project UNITY, funded by the Common Health Coalition.
Who can apply?
Youth-led nonprofit organizations focused on public health, medicine, or digital health that are past the idea phase but need operational support to scale.
How long is the program?
6 months. Organizations work with mentors throughout, with milestones for mission refinement, operations, grant disbursement, and a final showcase.
Do organizations receive funding?
Yes. Each organization accepted into Y-PHIC receives a grant to help them scale their work.
How is Y-PHIC different from Catalyst Academy?
Catalyst Academy is for individual high school students focused on research and learning. Y-PHIC is for existing youth-led nonprofit organizations that need support to scale.
