Our Three Pillars

What We Do

Education

Through Catalyst Academy and bootcamps, we deliver curriculum that bridges public health theory with hands-on research.

Mentorship

Every student is paired with graduate-level mentors from leading institutions who guide research and career navigation.

Community Innovation

Through Y-PHIC and our intervention model, we support youth-led projects creating measurable, lasting impact.

Our Story

How It Started

Project UNITY began during the COVID-19 pandemic, when a group of pre-medical students recognized a critical gap in public health education. With infection rates rising and access to reliable information limited, they created nationwide webinars to educate high-risk communities on mental health, diabetes, heart disease, and nutrition.

The overwhelming response sparked a bigger vision. What started as a grassroots effort grew into a nonprofit with a clear mission: to educate and empower future leaders to bridge medicine, public health, and digital health to improve care and advance equity.

As healthcare evolves, so must the people who shape it. We believe a strong foundation in public and digital health is essential to addressing systemic challenges and creating lasting impact.

Working Across Three Fields

Our Disciplines

Public Health

Population-level thinking, epidemiology, health equity, social determinants, and community-based research.

Medicine

Clinical perspectives, patient care, evidence-based practice, and bridging population health to individual outcomes.

Digital Health

Health technology, AI in healthcare, data science, telehealth, and digital tools for equity and access.

Diversity, Equity, & Inclusion Statement

At Project UNITY, we are committed to building a diverse, equitable, and inclusive community. We actively engage individuals from diverse racial, cultural, professional, and socio-economic backgrounds, believing that diverse perspectives strengthen our impact. We strive to cultivate a team that reflects the communities we partner with - fostering trust, youth mentorship, and lasting relationships that inspire positive health outcomes.
