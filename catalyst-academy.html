Course Structure

8 Weeks of Intensive Learning

Sessions twice per week (2.5 hours each), plus readings and research. Expect 10-12 hours per week.

W1
Foundations of Public Health
Orientation & Core Concepts
Introduction to public health frameworks, health equity, and social determinants of health. Students form research teams and begin identifying community health challenges.
W2
Research Methods & Design
Building Your Research Foundation
Introduction to qualitative and quantitative research methods, study design, literature reviews, and ethical research practices.
W3
Data, AI & Digital Health
Technology Meets Healthcare
Exploring how data science, AI, and digital health tools are reshaping healthcare delivery and advancing health equity.
W4
Medicine & Clinical Perspectives
Bridging Public Health & Clinical Care
Guest speakers from leading medical institutions discuss the intersection of public health and clinical medicine.
W5
Community Engagement
From Research to Action
Learning community-based participatory research approaches. Student teams design evidence-backed health interventions.
W6
Writing & Publication
Academic Writing for the UNITY Journal
Academic writing workshops, peer review sessions, mentor feedback, and scientific communication best practices.
W7
Poster Design & Presentation
Preparing for the Conference
Scientific poster design, presentation coaching, practice sessions, and preparation for the UNITY Research Conference.
W8
Final Presentations
UNITY Research Conference
Final paper submissions, poster presentations, awards ceremony, and program completion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Have Questions?

Who is eligible to apply?
Catalyst Academy is open to all high school students in the United States and Canada. Applicants must be in 9th, 10th, 11th, or 12th grade during the application cycle.
Do I need prior experience?
No specific prior experience is required. Use your application to explain why public health interests you and what skills you hope to gain.
How much time per week?
Synchronous sessions twice per week (2.5 hours each), plus readings and assignments. Expect around 10-12 hours per week total.
What tools do I need?
A stable internet connection, a device with a webcam and microphone. Zoom, Google Classroom, and Slack are used throughout.
What is the UNITY Research Journal?
An open-access scientific journal that publishes the original research papers written by Catalyst Academy students each year.
What happens after the program?
Students execute 12-month health interventions with support, may complete internships, and join the lifelong Project UNITY network.

FAQ

Questions About Mentoring

Do I need teaching experience?
No. We look for passion for public health and subject-matter expertise. Project UNITY provides mentor training and structured curriculum materials.
What is the time commitment?
Approximately 5-8 hours per week over 8 weeks (June 15 - August 7, 2026), including two sessions per week and the UNITY Research Conference on August 8.
What support does Project UNITY provide?
Mentor training, detailed curriculum, session plans, access to the leadership team, and a community of fellow mentors.
How many students will I mentor?
Typically a team of 4-6 students who work collaboratively on a research project throughout the 8-week program.
