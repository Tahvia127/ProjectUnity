Who Are We?

A National Innovation Lab for the Next Generation

Project UNITY is a youth-led nonprofit transforming how the next generation prepares to lead. Founded in 2020, we operate as a national innovation lab where students and young professionals build solutions to urgent public health challenges. Through education, mentorship, and community innovation, we train leaders in public health, medicine, and digital health to shape the future of healthcare.

Collaborators from Leading Institutions
Common Health Coalition

Project UNITY Is a Proud Member

Founding Institutional Partner of Y-PHIC

Building the future of public health - together.

Project UNITY is a proud member of the Common Health Coalition, a national coalition of more than 300 organizations working to strengthen the bridge between health care and public health. The Coalition is the founding institutional funder of our Y-PHIC accelerator.
